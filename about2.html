About Us & Studio Heritage

Carrying forward an 800,000-year human tradition of stone carving with 21st-century CNC engineering, dual manufacturing facilities, and generational artisan mastery.

The GRAVA Philosophy

Where Ancient Tradition Meets Architectural Mastery

"The oldest known works of representational art are stone carvings. Often marks carved into rock or petroglyphs will survive where painted work will not. Prehistoric Venus figurines such as the Venus of Berekhat Ram may be as old as 800,000 years, and are carved in stones such as tuff and limestone."

At GRAVA - The Stone Art STUDIO (Athena Stones), we are deeply engaged in carrying forward this age-old tradition of mankind by expressing mood, nature, and the world around us through stone art.

Athena Carving is dedicated to working towards the exact need of Architects, Interior Designers, and Discerning Clients in providing the finest bespoke solutions as per their creative concepts and structural blueprints.

With a modest beginning from Ahmedabad, Gujarat, Athena Stones has expanded into two dedicated production units: one in Ahmedabad and our flagship manufacturing workshop situated in the stone handicraft capital at Amberi, Udaipur, Rajasthan.

Hereditary Craft

Master carvers preserving traditional Indian undercut and jharokha techniques.

Modern Precision

Computerized multi-axis CNC routers and high-pressure abrasive water jets.

Infrastructure & Capacity

Two State-of-the-Art Production Units

Equipped with advanced machinery and generational artisans, our dual facilities handle everything from architectural elevations to delicate hand-sculpted icons.
