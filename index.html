Introduction

The Enduring Legacy of Representational Stone Art

The oldest known works of representational art are stone carvings. Often marks carved into rock or petroglyphs will survive where painted work will not.

Prehistoric Venus figurines such as the Venus of Berekhat Ram may be as old as 800,000 years, and are carved in stones such as tuff and limestone. At GRAVA The Stone Art Studio (Athena Stones), we are deeply engaged in carrying forward this timeless tradition of mankind by expressing mood, nature, and the world around us through stone art.

Athena Carving is dedicated to working towards the exact needs of Architects, Interior Designers, and Clients in providing the finest solutions as per their creative concepts.

Technical Excellence

Our 4-Step Architectural Execution Process

How we transform raw stone boulders into breathtaking architectural installations for projects across India.

1

CAD & 3D Modeling

Our in-house design studio translates architect elevations and client visions into precise 3D digital models and CAD drawings.

2

Block Selection & Mediums

Hand-picking premium natural stones (Sandstone, Marble, Granite) and planning fusion elements with seasoned wood and metals.

3

CNC & Water Jet Carving

State-of-the-art CNC routers and high-pressure abrasive water jets carve complex contours and perforations with sub-millimeter precision.

4

Hand Artistry & Installation

Master hereditary artisans finish undercuts, textures, and polish by hand, followed by careful site co-ordination and fitting.
