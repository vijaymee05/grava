About Us & Studio Heritage

Carrying forward the timeless 800,000-year legacy of stone sculpting with cutting-edge CNC technology, dual production facilities, and generational artisan mastery.

The GRAVA Story

Where Ancient Tradition Meets Modern Architectural Precision

"The oldest known works of representational art are stone carvings. Often marks carved into rock or petroglyphs will survive where painted work will not. Prehistoric Venus figurines such as the Venus of Berekhat Ram may be as old as 800,000 years, and are carved in stones such as tuff and limestone."

At GRAVA - The Stone Art STUDIO (Athena Stones), we are passionately engaged in carrying forward this age-old tradition of mankind. We believe stone is not merely a construction material; it is an enduring canvas capable of expressing emotion, nature, and the world around us through timeless art.

Dedicated to working closely with Architects, Interior Designers, and Discerning Clients, Athena Carving provides bespoke, site-specific solutions tailored precisely to client concepts and architectural visions.

With a modest beginning from Ahmedabad, Gujarat, Athena Stones has expanded into two dedicated production units: one in Ahmedabad and our flagship facility in the heart of India's world-famous stone handicraft hub at Amberi, Udaipur, Rajasthan.
