800,000-Year Heritage

Carrying Forward Mankind's Oldest Art Form

"The oldest known works of representational art are stone carvings. Often marks carved into rock or petroglyphs will survive where painted work will not. Prehistoric Venus figurines such as the Venus of Berekhat Ram may be as old as 800,000 years, and are carved in stones such as tuff and limestone."

We at Athena Stones / GRAVA are engaged in carrying forward this age-old tradition of mankind by expressing the mood, nature, and the world around us through art. Athena Carving is dedicated to working towards the exact need of Architects, Designers, and Clients in providing the best solutions as per their Concepts.

With a modest beginning from Ahmedabad, Gujarat, Athena Stones has now two production units: one in Ahmedabad and the second in the hub of stone handicrafts at Amberi, Udaipur, Rajasthan. Both units are equipped with skilled artisans, CNC routers, and Water Jet cutting facilities.
